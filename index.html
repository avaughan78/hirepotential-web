Four tools, one flow

Everything between the job post and the interview.

Each piece works standalone, but they're built to feed each other — research a role once and it sharpens the CV score, the LinkedIn score, and the questions you rehearse.

01

Research a Role

Paste, upload, or link a job description. Get the interview themes hiding in the wording and a set of tailored competency questions.

02

CV Review

Scored on impact, wording, and formatting — matched against your role's keywords once one's active. No CV yet? A free guided checklist and builder link get you started.

03

LinkedIn Review

Structure, content, and — if you've added a CV — consistency between the two. PDF export, screenshots, or paste, whichever's easiest.

04

Rehearse Aloud

Answer real competency questions out loud, transcribed on-device, scored on content and structure — with specific, actionable feedback.

How it works

Three steps, in whatever order suits you.

01 / RESEARCH

Drop in a job description

Paste it, link it, or upload the file. HirePotential reads it once and keeps it active across every other tool in the app.

02 / SHARPEN

Check your CV and LinkedIn

See exactly where they match the role and where they don't — with specific, checkable suggestions, not a vague score.

03 / REHEARSE

Say your answers out loud

Real competency questions built from the role. Transcribed and scored, so you walk in having actually practiced, not just read.

Built to hold onto less, not more

No accounts. Nothing sold. Little kept.

HirePotential doesn't ask who you are before it helps you prepare.

  • No sign-in, no account, no advertising or analytics tracking.
  • Your role, CV, and review results stay on your device — not in a database we run.
  • LinkedIn profile content is used for the one check that needs it, then discarded.
  • In-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple — we never see payment details.
