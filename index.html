A score, not just a "looks good"

03 — LinkedIn Review

Checked against what a profile actually needs

Many employers check LinkedIn before they call. Get suggestions for each section of your profile, and a check that it's roughly in sync with your CV — not generic "add more keywords" advice.

How it works

Three steps, start to finish.

01 / ANALYSE

Drop in a job advert

Paste it, link it, or upload the file. HirePotential reads it once and keeps it active across every other tool in the app.

02 / SHARPEN

Check your CV and LinkedIn

See exactly where they match the role and where they don't — with specific, checkable suggestions, not a vague score.

03 / REHEARSE

Say your answers out loud

Real competency questions built from the role. Transcribed and scored, so you walk in having actually practiced, not just read.

Built to hold onto less, not more

We don't keep your data on our servers.

No account to create, nothing about you handed over — your CV, your role, and your results stay on your device.

  • No sign-in, no account, no advertising or analytics tracking.
  • Your role, CV, and review results stay on your device — not in a database we run.
  • LinkedIn profile content is used for the one check that needs it, then discarded.
  • In-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple — we never see payment details.
