Common questions

  • How does the paid unlock work? A one-time purchase (currently £8.99) unlocks unlimited Rehearse Aloud sessions, CV Review, and LinkedIn Review — no subscription. It's a standard Apple in-app purchase, so it's tied to your Apple ID and restorable on any device via "Restore Purchases" in Settings.
  • Something not working? Include your iOS version and, if possible, what you were doing when it happened — that's usually enough to track down.
  • Requesting a refund? Refunds for the in-app purchase are handled by Apple, not by us directly — see reportaproblem.apple.com.
